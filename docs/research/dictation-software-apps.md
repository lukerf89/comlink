# what are some common voice dictation software apps? thinking of wispr flow, granola, etc

A bunch of solid options exist in the “Wispr Flow / Granola” space, ranging from local Whisper-based apps to cloud dictation and OS-native tools.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

## Modern AI dictation apps

These are closest in spirit to Wispr Flow and Granola (quick voice → cleaned-up text for notes/emails).

- Wispr Flow – Cross-app dictation with AI cleanup, recommended by several reviewers as the default pick for general-purpose voice typing.[^2][^4]
- SuperWhisper – Desktop app built on OpenAI Whisper, popular as a fast, local alternative to Wispr Flow.[^5][^6][^1]
- Aqua Voice / Aqua – Lightweight dictation app for Mac/Windows, often listed as a top Wispr alternative, with a developer-friendly angle.[^7][^5]
- Willow Voice / Willow – General-purpose dictation focused on ease of use and cross-app typing.[^4][^1][^5]
- Voibe – Privacy-first app with 100% offline local processing and low resource usage, explicitly positioned as a Wispr Flow alternative.[^8][^3]
- Typeless – Frequently recommended for its generous free plan while still offering cross-app dictation.[^3][^5]
- VoiceInk – Another Wispr-style dictation tool, aimed at everyday writing and productivity.[^5][^4]


## Offline / local-first options

If you care about local Whisper, privacy, and working without a network:

- MacWhisper – macOS-only Whisper client; records locally and transcribes fully offline.[^6][^9][^1]
- Whisper.cpp-based apps – Multiple tools wrap whisper.cpp (e.g., Weesper Neon Flow and others) for offline dictation.[^6][^3]
- Voibe – Again here, because it’s explicitly offline/local and privacy-focused.[^8][^3]


## Meeting / note-taking oriented

Closer to Otter / Granola style “meetings + summaries” but still usable for dictation:

- Otter.ai – Real-time meeting transcription with summaries; also usable for solo dictation and note capture.[^9][^4]
- Sonix – Upload audio/video, get multi-language transcripts and editing tools.[^9][^4]
- Notta – Speech-to-text, transcription, and note-taking for meetings and calls.[^1][^3][^4]


## Built-in and general productivity tools

Not always marketed as “dictation apps,” but commonly used for voice typing:

- Microsoft Word / Office Dictate – Integrated speech-to-text in Office, well regarded in traditional dictation reviews.[^2][^4]
- Apple / macOS Dictation – System-level dictation available across apps; often used as a baseline.[^3][^2]
- Google Docs Voice Typing / Chrome dictation extensions like Dictanote / Dictote – Browser-based voice typing for text fields.[^4][^9]
- ClickUp Talk to Text – Voice typing tied into ClickUp’s workspace and “AI Brain”; useful if you live in ClickUp already.[^10][^9]


## Quick comparison

| App / category | Local/offline | Cross-app dictation | Focus area | Platforms (high level) |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Wispr Flow | Partial | Yes | General writing | Desktop/mobile[^1][^2] |
| SuperWhisper | Yes | Yes | Fast Whisper-based dictation | Desktop[^1][^5][^6] |
| Aqua Voice / Aqua | Some | Yes | Developer-friendly, productivity | Mac/Windows[^7][^5] |
| Willow Voice | No/partial | Yes | Everyday writing | Desktop[^1][^5] |
| Voibe | Yes | Yes | Privacy-first, low resource | Desktop[^8][^3] |
| MacWhisper | Yes | Limited | Local transcripts | macOS only[^1][^6] |
| Otter.ai | No | No (app-centric) | Meetings, summaries | Web/mobile[^9][^4] |
| Notta | No | Limited | Transcription/notes | Web/mobile[^1][^3] |
| Microsoft Dictate | No | Office apps | Traditional dictation | Windows/macOS[^2][^4] |
| ClickUp Talk to Text | No | In ClickUp only | Workspace-integrated notes | Web/desktop[^9] |

For your workflow (heavy coding, lots of browser and IDE usage), the usual short list people end up testing against Wispr Flow is SuperWhisper, Voibe, Aqua, Willow, and MacWhisper (if you’re Mac-only).[^1][^5][^3][^4]

Are you primarily looking for cross-app dictation while coding (IDE, terminal, browser), or more for capturing longer notes and meeting summaries?
